METHOD

Core strength

Network penetration testing, end to end.

The part of the job I'd do for free. The same loop, every engagement, whether it's a lab box or a production network. The terminal on the right is live: type help.

  1. Recon

    Map the attack surface: live hosts, open ports, service versions, trust relationships. Nothing gets exploited that wasn't enumerated first.

  2. Enumerate

    SMB, LDAP, SNMP, web stacks, routing and switching layers. Every service leaks something if you ask it the right way.

  3. Exploit

    A foothold through misconfiguration, weak credentials or a known CVE. Clean, logged, and inside scope.

  4. Escalate & move

    Privilege escalation, lateral movement and post-exploitation to show the real blast radius, not just a single shell.

  5. Report

    Findings scored with CVSS, reproduction steps a developer can follow, and fixes ranked by what reduces risk fastest.
